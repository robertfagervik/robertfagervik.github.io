Robert Fagervik

Business and Management student at the University of Bristol, dual British and Norwegian. I build financial models and write equity research, mostly around cost of capital, DCF and private equity underwriting.

I also ship the tools that go with the analysis. A model that only exists as a spreadsheet tends to sit unopened, so I have taken to putting them on the web where people can actually use them. Magnec started as an occupier cost model in Excel and ended up as a site comparison tool for a startup COO. The Signal is a news aggregation platform running on Next.js and PostgreSQL.

Alongside that I am building a student housing venture in Bristol. Students sign twelve month contracts on very little information and generally find out what they have agreed to after moving in. I am working on changing that, and it has turned into the project I spend most of my spare time on.

I'm always happy to talk more about my experiences and learn from other people. Please don't hesitate to get in touch. My email and LinkedIn are at the bottom of the page.
